I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at [ETH Zurich](https://ethz.ch/en.html){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, working in the [Computer Vision and Geometry Group](https://cvg.ethz.ch/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} supervised by [Prof. Xi Wang] (https://xiwang1212.github.io/homepage/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and [Prof. Marc Pollefeys](https://people.inf.ethz.ch/pomarc/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

During my studies, I did an exchange at [University of Washington](https://www.washington.edu/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} and was a student researcher working with [Prof. Noah A. Smith](https://nasmith.github.io/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. I completed my Bachelor's in Computer Science at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}.

My research interests lie in 3D computer vision and robotics, with a focus on spatial memory and 3D understanding to help robots perceive and interact with the world.
